QUAN LY NHAC
1. Mo Supabase Dashboard > SQL Editor.
2. Mo tep sql/CAI-DAT-NHAC-NOEL.sql, dan noi dung va Run mot lan.
3. Dang nhap admin.html bang tai khoan Admin.
4. Trong muc “Nhac nen Giang Sinh”, chon MP3/WAV/OGG va bam “Tai nhac moi len”.
5. Khach truy cap chi co nut phat/tam dung va dieu chinh am luong; khong co chuc nang tai nhac len.

Luu y: Trinh duyet thuong chan tu dong phat am thanh khi khach chua tuong tac. Khach chi can bam nut phat nhac; khong can tai len hay them tep nhac. Sau khi chay SQL, Admin co the quan ly nhac dung tren Supabase Storage.

LUU Y VE TU DONG PHAT
Mot so trinh duyet (dac biet iPhone/Safari) khong cho phep tu dong phat nhac khi nguoi dung chua cham vao trang. Nut phat nhac se hien o goc duoi; khach chi can bam mot lan de nghe. Nhac da duoc dong goi san, khong can khach them tep.
